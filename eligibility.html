Donation Guidelines

Age

Minimum age should be 18 years and maximum 65 years.

Weight

Weight should be at least 50 Kg.

Health

You should be healthy and free from serious illness.

🩺 Health Tips Before Donating

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water before donating blood.

🍎 Eat Healthy

Have a light and healthy meal before donation.

😴 Good Sleep

Take at least 7–8 hours of sleep before donating.

🚭 Avoid Smoking

Avoid smoking or alcohol before and after donation.

About Blood Bank

What is Blood Bank?

A Blood Bank is a healthcare facility where blood is collected, tested, stored and supplied to hospitals for patients in need. It ensures safe and timely blood availability during emergencies.

Why Donate Blood?

One blood donation can save up to three lives. Blood cannot be manufactured, so voluntary donation is the only way to help patients during surgeries, accidents and diseases.
